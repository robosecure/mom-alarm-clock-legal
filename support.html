Mom Alarm Clock Support

Support for the public iPhone and iPad version

Need help? Email momalarmclock@gmail.com. Include the iPhone/iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. Do not send a real child's name, school, schedule, family code, password, or other sensitive information.

Before setup

  • The paired workflow needs a separate parent Apple device and child Apple device.
  • Install Mom Alarm Clock on both devices, choose the correct role on each, then follow the in-app pairing flow.
  • Both devices need internet access for pairing and remote sync.
  • The Android app is in testing through a tester programme. General availability will follow.

If an alarm or notification does not appear

  • On the child device, open Settings → Notifications → Mom Alarm Clock and confirm notifications and sounds are allowed.
  • Check the child's ringer, alert volume, Focus, and Do Not Disturb settings.
  • Open the app on both devices and confirm the expected child, alarm time, active days, and roles.
  • When contacting support, describe whether the alarm failed to appear, appeared without sound, or completed without updating the parent view.

Mom Alarm Clock is not an emergency or safety-critical alarm service. Mobile operating-system settings, connectivity, and device state can affect delivery.
