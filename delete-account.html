Delete your Mom Alarm Clock account

For the iPhone, iPad and Android apps

Google Play and the App Store require a way to request account deletion outside the app. If your app has Settings > Delete Account, use it. If it does not (the current test versions on iPhone, iPad and Android), request deletion on this page.

How to request deletion

Email momalarmclock@gmail.com with this subject line: 
Delete my account

What to include

The apps do not show an account or family ID, so we need these details to find your family:

  • Each child’s name, as shown in the app
  • The phone or tablet model of each device in the family
  • The approximate date the family was created
  • If you added one: the email address used to sign in

How we confirm the request

  • Accounts with an email address (iPhone and iPad): we reply to your request and confirm it with the email address on the account.
  • Accounts without an email address (all Android accounts, and iPhone or iPad accounts that never added one): we send you a short code; rename an existing alarm on the parent’s phone to that code. If you have no alarms, tell us and we will agree another way to confirm.

What is deleted

The family’s data includes:

  • The parent and child profiles
  • Alarms and morning sessions
  • Tamper events
  • Voice recordings and voice messages
  • Push notification tokens and push logs
  • Join codes
  • Analytics events tied to the family
  • The sign-in account itself (Firebase Authentication)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept

  • Backups: backups are kept for seven days. Deleted data is removed from backups as those backups age out, within seven days.
  • Email lists: if you joined a waitlist or product-update list, that data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, subject to limited records needed to document consent, suppression, security, or legal obligations.

When it happens

We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming the request, and reply to tell you it is done.
