Delete your Mom Alarm Clock account

For the iPhone, iPad and Android apps

Google Play and the App Store require a way to request account deletion outside the app. This page describes what the Android app deletes by itself, and how to ask us to delete anything else.

The current test versions on iPhone and iPad: request deletion by email, as described below.

In the Android app

These controls are on the parent’s phone. Android has no sign-in email; the account is tied to the phone the app is installed on.

  • Delete alarm (on each alarm): the alarm is turned off, and the app waits until the child’s phone confirms the alarm is gone. Only then are the alarm and its morning history deleted. If the alarm changed in the meantime, nothing is deleted and the app says “Couldn’t remove — the alarm changed; delete it again.”
  • Remove (on each child): every alarm of that child is turned off, and the app waits until the child’s phone confirms the alarms are gone. Then the child’s profile, alarms, morning history, tamper events and reward redemptions are deleted. The child’s Android phone then resets itself and shows “This phone was removed from the family.”
  • Delete account and all data (in Account, at the bottom of the parent’s home screen): deletes the family, every child’s profile, all alarms, morning history, tamper events, rewards, reward redemptions and recorded voice messages, then deletes the sign-in account. It is not available while an alarm is going off or a wake-up check is open on that phone. It turns off alarms on the parent’s phone only; alarms already set on other phones may still ring until those phones find the family gone. A child’s Android phone then resets itself and shows “This phone was removed from the family.”
  • Remove this device (on a child’s phone): stops alarms on that phone. It does not delete the family’s data.

If a child uses an iPhone or iPad, that device also turns off removed alarms before anything is deleted, and resets itself when the child or the family is removed.

What the Android app does not delete

To have these deleted, email us as described below.

  • After Remove: the records of which phone holds each alarm, the child’s alarm-removal records, the family’s recorded voice messages, and the record of the removal itself.
  • After Delete alarm: the records of which phone holds the alarm, the alarm-removal records and the record of the removal itself.
  • After Delete account and all data: each phone’s membership record (the family it belonged to, its role, and its notification token if one was saved), the records of which phone holds each alarm, the family’s alarm-removal records, the records of earlier removals, and join codes.

If an iPhone or iPad is in the family, its bedtime settings, message settings and morning voice messages are also not deleted by the Android app.

How to request deletion

Email momalarmclock@gmail.com with this subject line: 
Delete my account

What to include

The apps do not show an account or family ID, so we need these details to find your family:

  • Each child’s name, as shown in the app
  • The phone or tablet model of each device in the family
  • The approximate date the family was created
  • If you added one: the email address used to sign in

How we confirm the request

  • Accounts with an email address (iPhone and iPad): we reply to your request and confirm it with the email address on the account.
  • Accounts without an email address (all Android accounts, and iPhone or iPad accounts that never added one): we send you a short code; rename an existing alarm on the parent’s phone to that code. If you have no alarms, tell us and we will agree another way to confirm.

What is deleted

When we delete a family on request, the family’s data includes:

  • The parent and child profiles
  • Alarms and morning sessions
  • Tamper events
  • Voice recordings and voice messages
  • Push notification tokens and push logs
  • Join codes
  • Analytics events tied to the family
  • The sign-in account itself (Firebase Authentication)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept

  • Backups: backups are kept for seven days. Deleted data is removed from backups as those backups age out, within seven days.
  • Email lists: if you joined a waitlist or product-update list, that data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, subject to limited records needed to document consent, suppression, security, or legal obligations.

When it happens

For a request by email, we complete deletion within 30 days of confirming the request, and reply to tell you it is done.
